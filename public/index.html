// the problem

Notes, revision, and deadlines all live in different, unreliable places.

Most students patch together their whole academic term from scattered chats and screenshots — and none of it is searchable when the exam is tomorrow.

01 · discovery

Finding the right notes is a scavenger hunt

No way to filter by subject code or quality — just scrolling chat history and hoping someone shared the right file.

02 · consumption

Turning notes into revision material is manual

Making summaries, flashcards, or practice questions from a 40-page PDF the night before an exam eats the time you don't have.

03 · deadlines

Deadlines live nowhere near the material

Sticky notes and memory track submissions and exam dates, disconnected from the notes you'd actually study from.
