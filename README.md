* **Learnora: Learn Together**

Problem Statement:- \
--Students across campuses struggle to find organized, subject-specific study material and often rely on scattered WhatsApp groups, Google Drive links, or unreliable sources for notes. There's no centralized, community-driven platform where students can share and discover notes by subject code, get AI-powered help to quickly digest that material (summaries, flashcards, quizzes), and stay on top of academic deadlines — all in one place.

Students today face three main problems:\
--Discovery problem — Finding quality notes for a specific subject/subject code (e.g., BACHY101) is inefficient. Students waste time scrolling through disorganized chat groups or asking around, with no way to filter by campus, subject, or note quality.

--Passive consumption problem — Once notes are found, converting them into exam-ready material (summaries, revision points, practice questions) is entirely manual and time-consuming, especially under deadline pressure.

--Deadline-tracking problem — Academic deadlines (assignments, submissions, exams) are tracked informally — sticky notes, memory, scattered reminders — leading to missed or last-minute submissions, especially when note-sharing and deadline info live in different places entirely.

Solution:-\
--Learnora is a campus-based notes-sharing platform where subjects function as dedicated communities, identified by subject codes for precise, structured discovery. Students upload notes to their campus's subject communities, where peers can browse, upvote, and engage with them. An integrated AI Study Buddy scans any uploaded note and, on request, generates concise summaries, flashcards, or quizzes — turning static notes into active revision tools instantly. A built-in calendar and reminders page lets students track assignment deadlines and exam dates, keeping academic organization and study material in a single ecosystem.

Objectives:\
--Centralize note-sharing by campus and subject code for faster, more relevant discovery\
--Reduce manual effort in exam prep through AI-generated summaries, flashcards, and quizzes\
--Encourage peer-driven, community-style collaboration (upvotes, subject communities) to surface high-quality content\
--Help students stay on top of deadlines with an integrated calendar/reminder system

Target Users:-\
--Primary: College/university students (especially first-year to final-year UG students) looking for reliable, peer-shared, subject-specific notes\
--Secondary: Students preparing for exams who want quick revision aids (flashcards/quizzes) without manually creating them\
--Tertiary: Study groups / class representatives who currently informally distribute notes and could formalize it through campus-based communities\
--Institutional angle: Colleges/coaching institutes that want a structured, moderated alternative to unregulated WhatsApp/Telegram note-sharing
